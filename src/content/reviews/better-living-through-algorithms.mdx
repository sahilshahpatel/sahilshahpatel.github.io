What I loved most about this story is how differently I took it than others in my discussion group. I love a story than can be interpreted multiple ways depending on the avenue of focus and this one certainly meets that criteria.

I, for example, did not consider this to be a story about AI. For me, that was largely irrelevant and just served as a black-box explanation of the premise. For others though, it was the whole point, and that produced very different feelings about the story as a while. I also loved the ambiguous ending, which left me feeling neither like things were better or worse off. And the exploring how technology connects and disconnects us feels especially relevant to me now having made most of my friends through initial online platforms.

At this point I am quite tired of Marvel movies, but I will still always look forward to a Spider-Man film, especially when it's centered on him. I think his coming of age story is the right amount of heartfelt and tender to me in a way that most superheroes aren't anymore.

The ending of the last film in this series had MJ forgetting who Peter was, and this film was tasked with joining them again. That could have happened in a bunch of ways, but I was very happy to see them specifically avoid a magic kiss. And even more than that I was really glad they tackled the true depth of something like this: that MJ would have made a new life for herself in this time, that the memories would be one sided when they met again, and that she would be angry at him for letting her go in that way. The ending having a "magic handshake" with Ned was actually completely acceptable to me, because they showed the two connecting again, and because friendship is more forgiving than romance.

I also think tieing Peter's story to Gene Gray's was really smart. To me this movie was a bit of a Zuko Alone, but Peter Alone, where he has to understand the value of human connection and the pitfalls of loneliness. That has always been Gene Gray's story, and so to recognize that and join the two as foils for one another was a level of story-writing quality I didn't expect from Marvel.

The movie isn't necessarily perfectly up my alley, but it was still really good, and definitely enjoyable.

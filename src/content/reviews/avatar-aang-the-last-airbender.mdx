[Avatar the Last Airbender](avatar-the-last-airbender) has not had a particularly great history of adaptations and sequels. I am more partial to [Korra](the-legend-of-korra) than many, but I cannot deny that no complete Avatar story has captured the magic od the first series. It's with that context (and the more immediate context of the horrible Netflix adaptation) that I came to this movie with low expectations. To be honest, I forgot it was even being made until Zach sent me the trailer. From that I saw the beauty of the animation and decided to give it a chance.

Ultimately, this movie is pretty good, but certainly not amazing. I would rate it similar to the comics in that it expands on characters and a world that we love in a very interesting way, but it doesn't quite capture the magic.

I was surprised that they decided to write another story featuring evil spirit demons. But ultimately it was done a lot better in this movie than in Korra. I expected the constraints of the story (since it takes place between the original and Korra) to be a burden, but it actually worked quite well. The ending felt natural and also fit very well into the lore established in Korra.

I really can't complain about the movie much. It's not all I could have hoped for, but it's none of what I feared either, and I can be content with that.
